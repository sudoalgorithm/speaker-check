Speaker Check

Acoustic end-of-line style test for phone speakers: plays test signals, listens with the microphone, and reports what a service technician would check.

Setup

  1. Case off. Silent off, Bluetooth off, no headphones. Volume ≈ 70% (the app also checks whether volume is too high).
  2. Phone flat on a hard table, screen up, portrait. Quiet room. Hands off the grilles.
  3. Allow the microphone when asked. Audio is analysed on the phone only; nothing leaves the device.

All measurements
Not measured (and why)
  • Impedance, DC resistance, Thiele/Small parameters — need electrical access to the voice coil; not possible from software.
  • Absolute SPL / sensitivity — the browser gives no calibrated level, so all levels are relative. Compare against your saved baseline instead.
  • Speaker polarity — needs a reference signal path or a second synchronised mic.
  • Stereo crosstalk — one microphone next to both speakers cannot separate leakage from the other channel.
  • Exact L/R balance in single-phone mode — the mic is beside one speaker; use two-phone mode or the ear test.

Previous runs

Manual tone (ear test)

Sweep slowly through 80–400 Hz at a medium volume. Normal: gets quieter at the low end but stays clean. Fault: buzz, crackle, or a vibrating-part sound at specific frequencies.

Two-phone mode

One phone plays, a second phone listens 10–15 cm from the speaker under test. Open this page on both. This mode measures left/right balance properly and avoids the mic-next-to-speaker bias.
